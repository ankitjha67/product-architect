# Southeast Asia Compliance Deep-Dive

> **⚠️ DISCLAIMER:** Laws change. This reflects early 2026 status. Always verify
> with local legal counsel before relying on any specific requirement.

## Singapore
- **PDPA (2012, amended 2020/2021):** Consent (deemed/notified/legitimate-interests exceptions added 2020), purpose limitation, notification, access/correction. **DPO mandatory** (publish contact). **Mandatory data breach notification** to PDPC within **3 calendar days** if significant scale (≥500 individuals) or significant harm; affected individuals notified too.
- **Data Protection Trustmark; cross-border transfer** allowed with comparable-protection safeguards (contracts/BCRs); APEC CBPR participant. No hard localization.
- **Do Not Call (DNC) Registry:** check before telemarketing calls/SMS/fax; consent + sender ID for marketing messages. Penalties up to **S$1M** (PDPA breach), recklessness offences.
- **Payments — MAS, Payment Services Act 2019 (PSA):** activity-based licensing. License classes: **Money-Changing**, **Standard Payment Institution** (below thresholds), **Major Payment Institution** (exceeds S$3M/month per activity or S$6M aggregate, or >S$5M e-money float). Activities: account issuance, domestic transfer, cross-border transfer, merchant acquisition, e-money issuance, digital payment token (DPT/crypto), money-changing. **DPT (crypto)** also under PSA + Financial Services and Markets Act consumer measures (no retail incentives, custody rules). AML/CFT (MAS Notices), travel rule.
- **Other financial:** Securities & Futures Act (CMS licence), Financial Advisers Act, robo-advisory guidelines; **MAS Sandbox / Sandbox Express**.
- **Tax:** Corporate **17%** (partial exemptions; startup tax exemption scheme). **GST 9%** (raised 2024); GST on imported digital services + low-value goods (OVR regime, register if >S$1M global + S$100K to SG). No capital gains tax; 90+ tax treaties.
- **Corporate — ACRA:** incorporate via BizFile (Pte Ltd); **≥1 resident director** (citizen/PR/EP), company secretary within 6 months, registered office, annual return + AGM, nominee-director common for foreigners.
- **Employment — MOM:** **Employment Pass (EP)** min qualifying salary **S$5,600+** (S$6,200 financial sector) + COMPASS points framework; **S Pass** (S$3,300+, quota + levy); Work Permit (low-wage, quota/levy). **Fair Consideration Framework** (advertise on MyCareersFuture first); CPF mandatory for citizens/PRs (employer up to 17%). Tripartite guidelines (TAFEP) on fair employment; workplace fairness legislation 2024.
- **E-commerce/consumer:** CCCS (competition + consumer protection / fair trading), Spam Control Act (unsolicited bulk email/SMS — unsubscribe + sender ID).
- **Quick facts:** common APAC HQ; English-language law; strong IP protection; UEN registration; GST-registered if OVR threshold met.

## Indonesia
- **PDP Law (No. 27/2022):** GDPR-inspired. Consent or other legal bases, **DPO** for high-risk/large-scale/public-service processing, data subject rights. **Breach notification 3×24 hours** to the supervisory authority + affected subjects. Transitional period ended Oct 2024; implementing regulation + the data-protection agency still being established — **verify enforcement**. Fines up to **2% of annual revenue**.
- **Data localization:** **public-sector / electronic system operators (PSE)** in strategic categories and financial data face localization; private PSEs may transfer abroad with adequacy/safeguards. **PSE registration with Kominfo (Komdigi)** mandatory for any platform serving Indonesian users — non-registration risks blocking. GR 71/2019 + MR 5/2020 (content takedown 24h / 4h urgent).
- **Payments — OJK + Bank Indonesia:** OJK licenses fintech P2P lending, ITSK; Bank Indonesia for payment systems (PJP/PIP licences, e-money). **QRIS** national QR standard. P2P lending tighter rules (rate caps, age limits) post-2024. **BI 7-day data localization** for payment-system data.
- **Tax:** Corporate **22%**. **VAT 11%** (rising to 12%). Non-resident digital-service providers must collect **11% VAT (PMSE)**.
- **Investment — BKPM/OSS:** foreign investment via **PT PMA**; **Positive Investment List (Perpres 10/2021)** replaced the negative list — most sectors open to 100% foreign, with conditions; minimum capital IDR 10B paid-up generally. OSS risk-based licensing.
- **Employment — Omnibus/Job Creation Law (Law 6/2023):** fixed-term + outsourcing rules; **province/sector minimum wage**; **severance substantial** (up to ~9+ months for long tenure, plus service-pay & compensation). BPJS health + employment social security mandatory. Foreign workers need RPTKA + work permit (IMTA) + local-understudy obligations.
- **E-commerce:** GR 80/2019; Permendag 31/2023 (social-commerce/e-commerce separation, min import price US$100 on cross-border).

## Thailand
- **PDPA (2019, fully enforced June 2022):** GDPR-style. Consent or lawful basis, **DPO** for large-scale/sensitive/monitoring, data subject rights, **breach notification 72 hours** to PDPC. Cross-border transfer requires adequate protection / safeguards (PDPC guidance). Fines: administrative up to **THB 5M** + criminal/punitive for sensitive data.
- **Payments — Bank of Thailand (BOT):** Payment Systems Act; e-money/payment licences. **PromptPay** national scheme. **SEC** for securities + **digital assets (Emergency Decree on Digital Asset Businesses)** licensing.
- **Tax:** Corporate **20%** (SME reductions). **VAT 7%**. Non-resident **e-service VAT 7%** (register if >THB 1.8M).
- **Foreign Business Act (FBA):** **List 1** prohibited, **List 2** (national security/culture — cabinet approval), **List 3** (Thais not ready — Foreign Business Licence). Foreign = >49% foreign-held; nominee shareholding illegal. BOI promotion can grant 100% foreign ownership + incentives.
- **Employment — Labour Protection Act:** **province minimum wage** (~THB 330–370/day); **severance 30–400 days** by tenure; work permit + visa (often 4 Thai:1 foreigner ratio).

## Vietnam
- **PDPD (Decree 13/2023, in force July 2023) + Personal Data Protection Law (effective ~2026 — verify):** consent + processing principles, **Data Processing Impact Assessment** and **cross-border transfer dossiers filed with the Ministry of Public Security**, data-subject rights, DPO/department for sensitive/large-scale.
- **Cybersecurity Law 2018 + Decree 53/2022:** **data localization** for in-country domestic + certain user data; foreign providers (social media, e-commerce, online services) that violate and fail to comply may be required to **store data in Vietnam and establish a local branch/representative office**.
- **Payments — State Bank of Vietnam (SBV):** intermediary payment service licences; **NAPAS** switch; Decree 52/2024 on non-cash payments; e-wallet KYC.
- **Tax:** Corporate **20%**. **VAT 8–10%** (temporary reductions). **Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT)** on cross-border services/digital supplies; non-resident e-commerce/platform tax registration portal.
- **Employment — Labour Code 2019:** region-based minimum wage (Regions I–IV); compulsory social/health/unemployment insurance (employer ~17.5%/3%/1%); work permits for foreigners (with exemptions). Overtime caps; severance/job-loss allowance.
- **E-commerce:** Decree 52/2013 + 85/2021 — platform registration/notification with MOIT.

## Philippines
- **Data Privacy Act 2012 (RA 10173):** **NPC** enforces. **DPO mandatory**; register data processing systems (PIC/PIP) with NPC if thresholds met. **Breach notification within 72 hours** to NPC + affected individuals. Extraterritorial. Penalties: imprisonment + fines.
- **Payments — Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP):** EMI / Operator of Payment System (OPS) registration/licensing; **InstaPay & PESONet** rails; National Retail Payment System. AMLC for AML/CFT; e-wallet KYC tiers.
- **Securities:** SEC (registration, crowdfunding, investment solicitation); SEC also registers all corporations.
- **Tax:** Corporate **25%** (**20%** for domestic SMEs with net taxable income ≤₱5M and total assets ≤₱100M, under CREATE). **VAT 12%**; **VAT on Digital Services Act (2024)** — 12% on non-resident digital service providers (register). Withholding taxes.
- **Investment:** Foreign Investments Act + **Foreign Investment Negative List**; many activities require 60% Filipino ownership (constitutional limits on land, media, public utilities); recent liberalization (Public Service Act, Retail Trade) opened telecom/retail; minimum capital US$200K for domestic-market enterprises (lower if advanced tech/employs 15+).
- **Employment — Labor Code:** strong **security of tenure** (just/authorized causes, due process; regularization after 6-month probation); **13th-month pay** mandatory; **regional minimum wage** (NCR ~₱610/day); SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG mandatory; substantial separation pay.

## Malaysia
- **PDPA 2010 + 2024 Amendment Act (phased in 2025):** now includes **mandatory DPO appointment**, **breach notification to the Commissioner (72h)** + affected individuals, **data portability**, biometric = sensitive data, controller↔processor "data user/processor" terminology aligned, and replacement of the closed whitelist with adequacy/safeguards for cross-border transfer. **No hard localization.** Personal Data Protection Commissioner enforces. **Verify which 2024 provisions are in force.**
- **Payments — Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM):** Financial Services Act / Islamic FSA; e-money & payment-system operator approval; **DuitNow QR** national standard; digital-bank licences issued 2022–2024. Securities Commission for capital markets / digital assets / equity crowdfunding / P2P.
- **Tax:** Corporate **24%** (SME 15%/17% tiered on first chargeable income). **SST** (Sales 5–10% / Service 6–8%, expanded 2024–2025); **Service Tax on imported digital services 8%** (register if >RM500K). **E-invoicing (LHDN MyInvois)** phasing in by turnover 2024–2026.
- **Investment:** generally liberal; some sectors have **bumiputera equity** conditions / sector licences (MITI, MDEC for tech/MSC status incentives).
- **Employment — Employment Act 1955 (2022 amendments):** **minimum wage RM1,700/month** (from 2025; previously RM1,500), **max 45 hrs/week**, expanded coverage (all employees, with overtime cap by salary), 7-day paternity / 98-day maternity leave, flexible-work requests, anti-forced-labour. EPF + SOCSO + EIS mandatory; foreign-worker levy + quotas.

## Consumer Protection & E-Commerce (region-wide)
- **Singapore:** Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act / "lemon law" for goods; CCCS enforcement; Spam Control Act.
- **Indonesia:** Consumer Protection Law 1999 + Permendag e-commerce rules; mandatory Bahasa Indonesia labeling; halal certification (BPJPH) for many products by 2024+.
- **Thailand:** Consumer Protection Act + Direct Sales/Direct Marketing Act (registration for online sellers); FDA labeling.
- **Vietnam:** Law on Protection of Consumer Rights 2023; e-commerce platform registration (MOIT); local-language requirements.
- **Philippines:** Consumer Act + Internet Transactions Act 2023 (e-marketplace duties, DTI registration, online business registry).
- **Malaysia:** Consumer Protection Act 1999 + Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations (seller disclosure); halal (JAKIM) for relevant products.
- **Advertising:** misleading/comparative-ad and influencer-disclosure norms exist across all six; alcohol/tobacco/financial-product ad restrictions vary.

## AML / KYC (region-wide)
- Every country has a financial intelligence unit + AML/CFT regime requiring KYC/CDD, beneficial-ownership, suspicious-transaction reporting: Singapore (MAS + STRO), Indonesia (PPATK), Thailand (AMLO), Vietnam (SBV AML dept), Philippines (AMLC), Malaysia (BNM FIED). FATF membership/APG mutual evaluations drive standards. Travel rule applies to crypto/VASPs in several.

## Common Edge Cases (Region-Wide)
- Serving Indonesian users with no local entity — **PSE registration with Komdigi/Kominfo is still required**; non-registration risks platform blocking.
- Storing Vietnamese user data only offshore — cyber-law localization + local-presence demands may apply to social/e-commerce/online services.
- Hiring foreign founder as Singapore director without EP — need resident director or EP; COMPASS/Fair Consideration apply.
- Treating Thai/Philippine staff as "easy to terminate" — strong security-of-tenure + mandatory severance/13th-month; misjudging is costly.
- Cross-border payment data — Indonesia (BI) and Vietnam push localization; segregate from non-payment data.
- "ASEAN as one market" assumption — no harmonized privacy/fintech regime; comply country-by-country. ASEAN Model Contractual Clauses help for transfers but aren't a single law.
- Nominee shareholding to dodge Thai FBA / Philippine ownership caps — illegal; use BOI/BOI-style incentives or legitimate structures instead (verify with counsel).
- Crypto/digital-asset offering across SEA — each country licenses separately (SG DPT under PSA, TH SEC digital asset, MY SC, ID Bappebti→OJK transition); no passporting.
- Marketing loans/BNPL — Indonesia (OJK rate caps + collection rules), Vietnam, Philippines all regulate consumer lending + debt-collection conduct.
- Severance underestimation — Indonesia/Philippines/Thailand have substantial statutory severance; model it into any layoff/restructure.
- Tax registration for non-resident digital sellers — ID (PMSE VAT), TH (e-service VAT), MY (service tax), PH (digital-services VAT 2024), VN (FCT/e-commerce portal) each require registration over thresholds.
